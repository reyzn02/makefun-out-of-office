A single painfully honest but still polite out-of-office auto-reply of at
most 200 characters, written by a program that prints it to
/output/entry.txt.

> I am out until Monday, and your email joins the queue I answer in order of who I would miss at the leaving do, so thank you for your patience while I rank my affections.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
